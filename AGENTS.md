# Marz Media website: guide for AI assistants and developers

Live site: https://www.marzmediaphotography.com (hosted on Netlify, project "marzmedia").
Every push to `main` on GitHub rebuilds and publishes the site in about a minute.

## How it fits together
- **Website pages**: generated by `site/build.mjs` (all page HTML and copy lives there).
  Netlify runs `node site/build.mjs "$COMMIT_REF" --live --local` and publishes `site/dist/`.
  Never edit `site/dist/` by hand; it is rebuilt on every deploy.
- **Prices**: `pricing.js` is the single source of truth (packages, à la carte by sq ft tier,
  add-ons, social reels plans, 5% tax). The booking page and the invoice function both read it.
- **Booking page logic**: `site/assets/book.js`. Styles: `site/assets/styles.css`.
  Shared behaviour (menu, gallery): `site/assets/site.js`.
- **Bookings back end**: Alex's original Wix site (ID 6ebb38fc-e522-4299-91ee-cffe513b6956) runs
  Wix Bookings, Google Calendar sync, the booking form and automations. Do NOT delete it or
  reconnect the domain to it. Service prices, add-ons and hours there should match `pricing.js`.
- **Invoices**: `netlify/functions/wix-booking.js` receives Wix automation webhooks and creates,
  updates, voids Stripe invoices (sent 7 AM on shoot day, due that day, itemized, 5% tax).
  Secrets (STRIPE_SECRET_KEY, WIX_WEBHOOK_KEY) live only in Netlify environment variables.
- **Photos**: served from Alex's Wix Media (static.wixstatic.com). The list is `site/catalog.tsv`.
- **Old/ignore**: root-level `index.html`, `booking*.html`, `portfolio.html`, `prep.html`,
  `style.css`, `files (1).zip`, `netlify/functions/check-availability.js`, `create-booking.js`
  are earlier prototypes and are not part of the live site. `site/gen.js` was for a retired Wix
  preview; keep it in step with build.mjs if convenient, but it is not deployed.

## Making a change
1. Edit `site/build.mjs` (pages/copy), `pricing.js` (prices) or `site/assets/*` (behaviour/look).
2. Check it builds: `node site/build.mjs test --live --local` (writes `site/dist/`).
3. Commit and push to `main`. Confirm the change on the live site after ~1–2 minutes.

## House rules (from Alex)
- No client-facing copy about payment timing ("nothing due today", deposits) or speed claims
  ("book in 2 minutes").
- Photo turnaround: next business day. Video: 24–72 hours.
- Same-day delivery requires an 8–9 AM start. 5% tax on every order.
- Brand palette: white, black, navy, grey. Keep it modern and luxury, mobile-first.
- Never put property addresses or locations on portfolio photos.
